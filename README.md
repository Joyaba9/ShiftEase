# ShiftEase

## Core Features:

### Business Registration:
  Getting started with our software is easy! Businesses create a profile that serves as the hub for managing schedules, roles and employees. An intuitive dashboard centralizes all of these tools for convenience.
  
### Logging In As An Employee:
  After a business initially creates an employee, the employee has a set username and password. The username is given to the employee, but follows the following convention; (business_id)U(emp_id). So for an employee with the id 60 and business id of 7, their username would be 17U60. Now for their initially created password, it follows the following convention; MMYYSSSS, where MM represents the month they were born, YY the year, and SSSS being the last 4 of their social security number. After initially logging in with this password, the system prompts the user to create a new one.
  
### Employee Management:
  With our system you are able to create custom roles to conform to your business needs, add employees, manage their availability, and grant permissions to both the business and employees as needed.
  
### Efficient Scheduling:
  Create weekly schedules quickly with our user-friendly interface. The system provides you easy access to creating and editing a schedule, showing you an employee's availability on the side.
  
### Shift Offering and Requests Management:
  Employees can post shifts they are unable or don’t want to work, allowing others to claim the very shifts. 
Managers can approve or deny vacation, sick time and even availability changes, ensuring adequate 
coverage. 

### Collaborative Tools and Notifications:
  Real-time notifications allow people to be informed on business needs, while notifying employees about 
schedule changes and approvals. There is also a messaging feature for employees to communicate within 
themselves.

This system transforms workforce management for small businesses. By combining simplicity with the 
ability to use robust tools, it helps foster collaboration and ensures smooth operation. It is perfect for any 
small retail shop, restaurant or growing business looking for hassle-free scheduling.
